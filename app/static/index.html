Help & Privacy

What this tool is

A community world-map viewer for Backyard Monsters Refitted (Map Room 2). Pan and zoom the map, search players, view profiles and leaderboards, share coordinate links, and browse any world's cached snapshot as a guest.

How it works

Map data comes from the game once, then is cached and shared: when anyone's viewer fetches a zone, everyone else sees it from the cache. Zones near your own bases refresh often (every 5 minutes); distant wilderness refreshes at most daily. A strict server-side budget caps total game-API traffic for all users combined, so the viewer can never hammer the game servers — distant areas may show data up to a day old, on purpose.

What we do with your data

Your password is never stored. Sign-in passes your credentials directly to the game and keeps only a session token, cached briefly in server memory for verification. Map data is public within this tool: base names, positions, levels, and main-yard avatars visible on the world map are cached and shown to all users, including guests, along with when each area was last observed. Alliance chat, targets, and feeds are stored on this viewer's server and visible to your alliance. Sign-in times are logged for moderation.

How to use it

Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, click a base for details. The toolbar has search, filters (including an inactivity filter), measurements, jump-to-coordinates, watch notifications, alliances, and the world picker — use it to browse other worlds from cache. Signed in, your own bases are always highlighted and the map around them stays freshest.

Want your base hidden from this viewer?

If this tool is being used to target or harass you, request hiding below. Approved players are camouflaged on the map and excluded from search, filters, profiles, and watch activity for normal users.
